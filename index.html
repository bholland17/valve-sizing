Evolut FX+ sizes by annulus perimeter and diameter with sinus of Valsalva minimums. All four sizes are listed on equal footing; read the entered measurement against the printed ranges. A sinus value is marked below only where it falls under that row's stated minimum. Oversize is perimeter oversizing — nominal valve perimeter divided by annulus perimeter — and is not interchangeable with the area oversizing shown for SAPIEN, which runs roughly double for the same fit. The 23 mm dagger values (53.4 mm / 17 mm) apply to TAV-in-SAV only, which is not approved in low- and intermediate-risk patients.

Reference aid only. Verify every value against the current instructions for use. Final sizing is a heart team decision that also accounts for calcium burden and distribution, left ventricular outflow tract anatomy, root angulation, coronary height, and access.
